# Identity assets

Generated by `npm run build`. Do not edit by hand.

## Marks

`perpeto-symbol.svg`, `perpeto-wordmark.svg` and `perpeto-lockup.svg` are byte-for-byte copies of `brand/identity/`. The `-black`, `-ink`, `-paper` and `-orange` variants change only the fill; path data is identical and tested.

- **Ink** on paper or white is the default.
- **Paper** is for ink surfaces.
- **Orange** is for the symbol at display sizes. Avoid the orange wordmark below 24 px tall: signal orange is about 3:1 on paper.
- Clearspace is at least one arm width on every side, about 8% of the symbol's width. The symbol SVG's own margin is smaller than that, so layouts must add it.

## App icons

Ink tile, paper Spindle, using the badge geometry from `brand/motion/variants/badge.svg` (mark at 68% of the tile).

- `app-icon/perpeto-app-icon-*.png`: full-bleed square for iOS and web. The system applies the mask.
- `app-icon/macos/perpeto-app-icon-macos-*.png`: Apple's macOS grid, 824 px rounded tile on a transparent 1024 canvas, no shadow.

## Favicons

`favicon/` has `favicon.svg` (ink, paper in a dark browser theme), `favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48), PNGs, `apple-touch-icon.png`, 192 and 512 icons, and `site.webmanifest`. All use the master Spindle.

## Small-size legibility

Rendered and inspected at 16, 24 and 32 px, ink on paper and paper on ink. See `inspection/legibility-sheet.png`: the top two rows are the master, the bottom two the proposal, each 8× enlarged next to actual size.

| Size | Master Spindle |
| --- | --- |
| 32 px | Clear. All eight arms and the central void read. |
| 24 px | Acceptable. Arms separate; the bends soften. |
| 16 px | Muddy. Arms merge into a grey pinwheel ring and the bends disappear. Reversed on ink it's worse. |

The app icons at 16 and 32 px (macOS list views) have the same problem, since the mark is smaller inside the tile.

## Proposal: simplified small Spindle

`proposed/perpeto-symbol-small-*.svg` is a **proposal for Justus, not an approved mark**. It keeps eight bent arms with the master's handedness on an exact 45° rotation, but uses thicker arms relative to size, shorter outer segments and a larger central void so each arm gets its own pixels at 16 px. At 16 px it reads as eight distinct hooks. At 32 px and above it looks thinner and more ring-like than the master, so it should only be used below 24 px.

`proposed/favicon-16-small.png` and `proposed/favicon-small.ico` (small 16, master 32 and 48) are ready to swap in if it's approved. Until then, the shipped favicon uses the master at every size.
