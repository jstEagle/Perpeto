Perpeto Mono

The product UI system for the web and Apple clients. Warm paper, white cards, near-black ink, and one small orange dot for work that is happening right now.

Minimal

One primary action per view. Secondary detail waits behind a disclosure.

Spacious

Monochrome

Rounded

Soft cards, pill buttons, continuous corners everywhere.

Smooth

Short ease-out motion that disappears under reduced motion.

Colour

Warm neutrals from paper to ink. The accent is a signal, not a theme.

Do and don't

The short version of docs/architecture/design-system.md.

Do

  • One black primary action per view.
  • Separate with whitespace and surface contrast.
  • Show three numbers; disclose the rest.
  • Pair every status with an icon and a word.
  • Keep titles large, short and sentence case.

Don't

  • Orange buttons, text, fills or borders.
  • Boxes inside boxes, heavy rules, zebra rows.
  • Uppercase headers, kickers or tracked-out labels.
  • Line grids, glows, gradients or animated dashes.
  • Mono for anything that is not an identifier.

Marks

Ink on paper in the product. Orange is for display-size brand moments.

Contrast

Every text pair meets WCAG AA at 4.5:1; icons, edges and boundaries meet 3:1. The build fails otherwise.
