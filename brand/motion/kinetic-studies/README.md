# Kinetic studies

[Interactive preview](preview.html)

Six motion explorations inspired by Dock. Existing arms translate only: no rotation, flipping, filling, added geometry, or opacity pulses.

- [Magnet](magnet.svg): Arms pull together, overshoot, and settle.
- [Rails](rails.svg): Two sets slide in from opposite sides.
- [Seat](seat.svg): Arms drop into position with a small mechanical bounce.
- [Piston](piston.svg): Opposing pairs extend and retract in sequence.
- [Interlock](interlock.svg): Each arm travels along two axes to find its seat.
- [Recoil](recoil.svg): A quick outward kick travels around the arms.

All SVGs respect reduced-motion preferences.
