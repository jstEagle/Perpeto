# Fill studies

[Interactive preview](preview.html)

The silhouette stays fixed. No rotation, flips, added visible shapes, strokes, or pulse effects. Clip shapes only control visibility within the existing paths.

- [Reservoir](reservoir.svg): The fixed mark fills from bottom to top.
- [Core](aperture.svg): Ink spreads from the center through the existing shapes.
- [Diagonal](diagonal.svg): A diagonal band of ink passes through the fixed silhouette.
- [Parting](parting.svg): A central reveal opens sideways through the mark.
- [Weave](weave.svg): Opposing pairs take turns coming into focus.
- [Register](register.svg): Arms switch on in sequence, hold, then clear.

Reduced-motion preferences restore the static mark.
