# Rhythm studies

[Interactive preview](preview.html)

No ink fills, rotation, flips, added shapes, or whole-logo pulses. Existing arms use opacity and small translations.

- [Gap](relay-gap.svg): A single quiet gap travels around the complete mark.
- [Packet](packet.svg): Opposite arm pairs switch on in precise steps.
- [Dock](dock.svg): Arms slide into position and gently settle.
- [Latch](latch.svg): Opposite pairs briefly disengage, then reconnect.
- [Ripple Step](bounce.svg): A small vertical step travels from arm to arm.
- [Rendezvous](rendezvous.svg): Opposite pairs appear in sequence, hold, and clear.

Self-contained SVGs, with reduced-motion support. New unselected explorations.
