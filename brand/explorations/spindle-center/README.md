# Spindle center refinements

Seven- and eight-arm raster concepts edited with the built-in image generation tool. Inner ends follow a circular aperture. These improve the visual center; exact rotational symmetry is not yet mathematically verified and remains a vector-production step.

## Seven arms

![Seven arms](seven.png)

Edit this 7-arm black logo. The specific required change is to the inner endpoints. Imagine a perfect white disk centered exactly at the rotational center, diameter about 36 percent of the total symbol diameter. Trim ALL black arms against that disk. Every arm's inward end must be a concave circular arc lying on that SAME circle, never a pointed tip or diagonal straight cap. The central negative space must visibly read as one clean round aperture, interrupted only by the equally spaced narrow white channels between arms. Reposition arms minimally to achieve exact 7-fold rotational symmetry and equal gaps. Keep EXACTLY 7 separate bent arms. Preserve their thickness, outer angled tips, softly rounded bends, direction of rotation and industrial silhouette. Crucial: remove the irregular star-shaped center and all inward points. Clearly round central aperture. No drawn circle outline, no hub, no added ring. Pure black on pure white, clean flat geometric logo, centered with generous margins. No text, no shadow, no gradients.
